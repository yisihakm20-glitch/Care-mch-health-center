About Our Centre

Care Maternal and Child Health Centre is dedicated to delivering world-class,healthcare services optimized for women, infants, and children. Situated in Jemo Michael, Addis Ababa, our facility combines cutting-edge clinical infrastructure with leading specialists in Obstetrics, Gynecology, Neonatology, and Pediatrics.

Our commitment encompasses complete patient-centered care—from clinical antenatal monitoring, specialized high-risk pregnancy control, advanced 4D imaging, safe delivery services, to pediatric subspecialty evaluations and immunizations.

Our Specialized Services

Antenatal Care (ANC) Follow-Up
Comprehensive maternal monitoring and clinical checks during all trimesters of pregnancy.
4D Ultrasound & Fetal Anatomical Scanning
High-resolution diagnostic sonography for detailed fetal development and anomaly evaluations.
Spontaneous Vaginal Delivery (SVD)
Safe, expert-guided natural labor and delivery supported by specialized midwife and obstetric care.
Elective & Emergency Cesarean Section (C-Section)
Fully equipped 24/7 surgical suites for planned and emergency surgical deliveries.
Postnatal Care (PNC)
Complete medical monitoring for mothers and infants immediately following delivery.
Neonatal Intensive Care Unit (NICU)
Advanced critical care for premature infants and medically compromised newborns.
General & Subspecialty Pediatric Outpatient Consultations
Routine health checks, growth assessments, and specialized pediatric medical care.
Routine & Catch-Up Childhood Vaccination
Full immunization schedules to safeguard children against infectious diseases.
Male Circumcision (Neonatal & Pediatric)
Sterile, safe, and minimally invasive clinical circumcision procedures for newborns and young boys.
Gynecological Outpatient Consultations
Expert evaluation and clinical management of reproductive health disorders.
Gynecological Surgeries (Minor & Major)
Complete range of surgical procedures for uterine, ovarian, and pelvic pathologies.
Gynecologic Oncology Consultations
Specialized consultation and management for gynecological malignancies and tumors.
Cervical Cancer Screening (Pap Smear & VIA)
Early detection screening programs for cervical precancers using modern techniques.
Family Planning & Contraceptive Services
Counseling and administration of long-term and short-term birth control options.
Infertility Evaluation & Management
Comprehensive diagnostic workup and tailored treatment options for couples.
24/7 Maternal & Pediatric Emergency Care
Round-the-clock emergency medical team ready to handle urgent conditions immediately.
Inpatient Admission & Ward Services
Clean, comfortable, and well-monitored inpatient rooms for complete recovery.
Comprehensive Diagnostic Laboratory Services
Fully automated clinical laboratory providing fast and precise diagnostic results.
In-House Pharmacy Services
Fully stocked pharmacy supplying high-quality pediatric and adult pharmaceuticals.
